# 3 missing and most needed features of Conductor summarized from happy
> Note: As required, try `codemap` first, but this command is not installed on this machine, so use `rg`/file scanning instead to complete the structural understanding and comparison of the two warehouses.
## 1) End-to-end encryption (E2E) and local key management
- **Happy evidence**: `<happy-repo>/sources/encryption/`, `<happy-repo>/sources/sync/encryption/`, `<happy-repo>/sources/text/_default.ts` ("End-to-end encrypted" copywriting).- **Why is it most needed**: The usage scenario of Conductor is "to safely control the local AI coding process without leaving the computer." Without E2E, the encrypted link and key are only on the client, and privacy and compliance risks will become the biggest obstacles to promotion and deployment.- **Conductor current gap**: No end-to-end encryption related implementation or protocol is seen (only regular HTTPS/crypto is used), and the client key and message encryption process are missing.
## 2) Push notification system (permission request/error/completion)
- **Happy evidence**: `<happy-repo>/sources/app/_layout.tsx` (Expo notification registration), `<happy-repo>/sources/sync/apiPush.ts` (push token registration).- **Why it is most needed**: The story setting of Conductor is that "the AI ​​is still running when the person leaves the computer". The user must be interrupted when the AI ​​needs human confirmation/error, otherwise the experience will be broken down by "missing key interactions".- **Conductor current gap**: Push registration and notification distribution have not yet been implemented at the code level (only as a future module in the document).
## 3) Real-time synchronization and session takeover of multiple devices (local ↔ remote seamless switching)
- **Happy evidence**: `<happy-repo>/sources/sync/` (session synchronization/device status/session control), `<happy-repo>/sources/app/(app)/new/index.tsx` (multi-machine/session selection).- **Why we need it most**: The core selling point of Conductor is "you can continue to collaborate even when you are away from the computer". If you cannot seamlessly take over the running session (including status, tool requests, log streams), the experience will be dragged down by the switching cost.- **Conductor current gap**: Although there is a session concept and WS, the complete implementation details or UI entry of "multi-device synchronization/takeover/visualization of device status" are not seen.
